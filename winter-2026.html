Winter backpacks 2026

At a Seattle bus stop, a man next to me wrung out his socks, put them in his bag, and pulled on a dry pair. We said nothing. I learned socks are basically gold.

Every dollar you give reaches into the moment someone opens one of our bags and thinks, “People cared enough to think about that?” And past that. To every choice when someone has enough to touch another life in need.
